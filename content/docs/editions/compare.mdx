---
title: Compare editions
---

Parseable is available in three editions: the free, [self-hosted OSS](https://github.com/parseablehq/parseable) core, the [fully managed Cloud](https://telemetry.new/) offering, and Enterprise for mission-critical deployments. This page compares what's included in each.

| | OSS | Cloud | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deployment | Self-hosted | Fully managed on Parseable infrastructure | Fully managed, BYOC, or On-Premises |
| Infrastructure | Self-managed | Shared infrastructure | Dedicated infrastructure |
| License | AGPLv3 | Commercial | Commercial |
